Privacy Policy

This page explains what WPS collects, what we do not collect, and how cookies work on this site.

Last updated: 16 October 2026 | WPS

What we collect

WPS uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how readers use the site. GA4 records aggregate, non-identifying data such as pages viewed, approximate country and city, device type, browser, and where a visit came from (for example search, social, or direct).

We do not ask you to log in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account details on this site. There are no forms, comment boxes, or user accounts.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits and to tell new visits from repeat visits. These cookies do not identify you by name. You can block cookies in your browser settings. If you block them, the site still works, but your visit will not be counted in analytics.

What we do not collect

We do not sell personal data. We do not run our own trackers beyond Google Analytics. We do not collect passwords, payment details, or private messages.

Advertising in the future

In the future WPS may show ads with Google AdSense. If AdSense is added, Google may use cookies to show ads and to measure them. This page will be updated before any ad system goes live, and the update date above will change.

Links to other sites

Articles link to public social posts in the What People Say box and may link to news sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies. WPS is not responsible for how those sites handle your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the last updated date above will change. The current version is always on this page.
