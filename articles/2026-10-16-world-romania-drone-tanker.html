Drone strike on tanker off Romania raises Black Sea shipping fears

A drone struck a chemical tanker off Romania's Black Sea coast and seven sailors are missing. A second tanker was also hit, raising fears over shipping safety.

16 October 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: Associated Press via The Manila Times, Reuters

Romanian authorities launched search and rescue operations off the Black Sea coast on Thursday after a drone struck a ship which then caught fire. The attack was the latest in a series of strikes that has raised fears that Russia's war in Ukraine is spreading.

Five people were rescued after the San Marino flagged Wine Trader, a chemical tanker travelling from Tunisia to Romania, caught fire in the attack about 13 nautical miles southeast of the port town of Sulina, according to the Romanian Naval Authority. Seven other crew members were still missing.

Romanian President Nicusor Dan said in Brussels that the cargo vessel had been struck by a drone, as was another ship in a separate incident the night before. He did not state where the drones came from, and said there would be a reaction from the Romanian state.

On Wednesday evening, a fire was also reported on the Comoros flagged Yara J, a chemical and oil tanker travelling from Turkey to Sulina, in Romanian territorial waters. The crew was safe. Romania's Ministry of National Defense said a group of air targets was monitored overnight near Ukraine's Snake Island, and that a drone briefly entered Romania's airspace at 1:34 a.m. southeast of Sulina before disappearing from radar.

The incidents follow drone strikes on two ships off the coast of Bulgaria on Tuesday. Eleven crew members from the HS Dignity, a Guinea-Bissau flagged cargo ship, remain missing, while rescuers evacuated all 15 crew members from the Chelsea 3, a Ukrainian operated freighter. Earlier, on October 5, a Turkish owned cargo ship carrying corn caught fire after a drone attack and later sank off Romania's coast, killing two people. NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte said he met with the Bulgarian and Romanian leaders to discuss security in the Black Sea, and that NATO had strengthened its posture along the eastern flank.
