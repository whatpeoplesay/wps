Macron calls Trump's migrant claims about France riots 'stupid'

Emmanuel Macron has fired back at Donald Trump over claims that migrants are behind the riots in France, calling such comments "stupid" and "completely false," a clash of presidents that has divided opinion online.

22 October 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: CNN, USA Today, Associated Press

French President Emmanuel Macron has called comments by foreign leaders about the riots in France "stupid," pushing back on claims that migrants are to blame for the unrest. He spoke on Wednesday, October 21, during a tour of Gulf states in the Middle East.

Read More

The rebuke followed claims by US President Donald Trump that France was being "conquered, looted and burned to the ground by migrants, jihadists and communists." Trump also said Paris would "be a Muslim city very soon" and urged Europe to close its borders and "take control, before it is too late."

"We've had people claiming that we were invaded by hordes of migrants and Islamists, which is completely false," Macron said. He added that there was "no visible foreign interference" in the unrest, but there had been "foreign manipulation of information" that led to "unacceptable and stupid comments from many people, including foreign leaders." Asked by a reporter if he meant Trump, Macron replied: "Among others."

The unrest began in September as protests over education funding and the cost of living, joined by students, labor unions, farmers and workers' groups. More than half a million people took to the streets on October 17, and some of the gatherings turned into riots and looting. More than 2,000 people were arrested over the weekend, and authorities imposed local curfews and promised a heavy police presence for more protests expected on Saturday.

Macron also pointed to fake AI-generated images being shared online that showed scenes which never happened in France. His foreign minister said France could give concerned countries the facts they need, so they stop spreading false claims on social media. Trump has continued to use the riots to argue for tougher immigration policies ahead of the US midterm elections.
