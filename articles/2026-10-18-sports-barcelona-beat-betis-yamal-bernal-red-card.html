Yamal stars as Barcelona beat Betis 5-2 after Bernal red card

Barcelona beat Real Betis 5-2 in LaLiga on Saturday, keeping their perfect start. Lamine Yamal scored the opener and set up another goal before a second-half clash left Betis with ten men.

18 October 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: NDTV, beIN SPORTS

Barcelona beat Real Betis 5-2 away in LaLiga on Saturday, keeping a perfect start of nine wins from nine. Lamine Yamal scored the opener and set up another goal, and the game turned on a second-half clash that left Betis with ten men.

Read More

Yamal put Barcelona ahead in the 10th minute. He turned past Facundo Bernal just outside the penalty area and curled a shot inside the far post. Betis levelled when Valentin Gomez headed in from a cross, but Rodri restored the lead in the 41st minute with a header from a corner. Betis thought they had equalised before the break, but Giovani Lo Celso's volley was ruled out for a handball in the build-up, and Betis manager Manuel Pellegrini was booked for his protests.

Six minutes after the restart, Yamal set up Gabriel Jesus for Barcelona's third goal. Two minutes later, Abde Ezzalzouli pulled one back for Betis to make it 3-2. Moments after that goal, Yamal and Bernal clashed off the ball.

Bernal moved his head toward Yamal, and the referee showed Bernal a red card and booked Yamal for his part in the incident. Betis played the rest of the match with ten men. Barcelona used the extra player quickly, with Eric Garcia scoring the fourth goal soon after, before Dani Olmo completed the 5-2 win late on.

The win keeps Barcelona on nine wins from nine in LaLiga. It matches the best start to a LaLiga season, a record set by Real Madrid in 1968-69. Barcelona have scored 39 goals in those nine league games. They now face Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League on Tuesday.
