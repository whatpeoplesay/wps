Sri Lanka loses power islandwide as grid trips, solar surplus blamed

Sri Lanka lost power across the whole island on Saturday morning after the national grid failed, possibly because solar power output rose above use. Supply was fully restored later the same day.

18 October 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: CNA, Ada Derana

Sri Lanka lost power across the whole island on Saturday morning after the national grid failed. The outage began at about 11:45 a.m. local time, and the country was without grid power for more than two hours.

Read More

The National System Operator said the grid suffered a total system failure after instability in the transmission network. Engineers worked to bring power back area by area, and the operator later said supply was fully restored across the island.

Officials said a possible cause was solar power generation rising above what people were using. The state-run Electricity Distribution Lanka said engineers were fixing a fault affecting the whole grid and suspected excess solar power had caused the breakdown.

Energy Minister Anura Karunathilaka said authorities hoped to restore supplies stage by stage. He asked owners of rooftop solar systems to disconnect their systems until further notice to support the restoration work.

It was the second such islandwide failure. In February 2025, the whole grid also shut down when solar output passed consumption on a bright Sunday, and it took more than six hours to restore supply. Sri Lanka has just over 3,000 megawatts of installed solar capacity, while daytime demand runs from 2,000 to 2,500 megawatts.
