Court rules Yes Bank AT1 bond write-off illegal, in a win for bondholders

The Supreme Court has ruled that the write-off of Yes Bank AT-1 bonds worth 8,400 crore rupees in 2020 was illegal and arbitrary. The decision offers relief to bondholders, but experts warn it could create regulatory uncertainty.

16 October 2026 | Business | By WPS Staff

Sources: TradingView (BL Morning Report), Reuters

The Supreme Court has ruled that the 8,400 crore rupee write-off of AT-1 bonds in 2020 was illegal and arbitrary. The ruling concerns Yes Bank and the decision to write off the bank's AT-1 bonds.

Read More

The write-off dates back to 2020, when the AT-1 bonds were written off. The court has now ruled that this write-off was illegal and arbitrary.

The decision offers relief to bondholders. The bondholders are the people who held the AT-1 bonds that were written off.

At the same time, experts warn the decision could create regulatory uncertainty. The warning is about what the ruling could mean for regulation going forward.

Despite the ruling, Yes Bank shares fell around 13 percent on Thursday. The share move came on the same day the ruling was reported.
