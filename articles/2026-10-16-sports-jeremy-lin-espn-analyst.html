Jeremy Lin joins ESPN as NBA analyst for 2026-27 season

Former NBA champion Jeremy Lin joins ESPN full time as a game analyst and NBA Today contributor, after covering the 2026 Finals and Summer League.

16 October 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: ESPN Press Room, Focus Taiwan / CNA

ESPN has named Jeremy Lin as part of its NBA analyst roster for the 2026-27 season. It is the network's 25th consecutive season of NBA coverage.

Read More

Lin will work as a game analyst and appear across ESPN's studio programming, including the weekday show NBA Today. He contributed to ESPN's coverage of the 2026 NBA Finals and NBA Summer League before joining full time.

Lin is a former NBA champion. He joins a game analyst group that also includes Richard Jefferson, Tim Legler, Doris Burke, Jason Kidd and P.J. Carlesimo.

Jason Kidd, a Hall of Fame point guard, also joins ESPN's coverage roster this season. He will work across game and studio coverage, including the new-look NBA Countdown show.

The lead broadcast team of Mike Breen, Tim Legler, Richard Jefferson and Lisa Salters returns for a second season together. The group will call the top regular-season games and lead ABC's coverage of the NBA Playoffs and NBA Finals. NBA Today, hosted by Malika Andrews, starts its fifth anniversary season with Lin among its contributors.
