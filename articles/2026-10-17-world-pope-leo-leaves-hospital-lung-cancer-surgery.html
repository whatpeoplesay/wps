Pope Leo leaves hospital four days after lung cancer surgery

Pope Leo XIV was discharged from Gemelli Hospital in Rome on Saturday, four days after surgery for lung cancer, and returned to the Vatican.

17 October 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: USA Today, The Hindu

Pope Leo XIV left hospital in Rome on Saturday afternoon and returned to the Vatican, four days after surgery for lung cancer. The 71-year-old walked unaided to his waiting car, stopping to speak to well-wishers outside Rome's Gemelli Hospital. He wore a smile as he left and waved to onlookers from his departure vehicle.

Read More

The pope had surgery on Tuesday, October 13, to remove the lower lobe of his right lung. Doctors had found a small nodule in a recent health checkup, and tests on the removed tissue confirmed it was lung adenocarcinoma, the most common form of lung cancer. His doctors have said the surgery was completed successfully and without complications.

On Saturday morning, his doctors Luigi Carbone and Stefano Margaritora said the pope's clinical condition was good enough to allow him to leave the hospital. They based the decision on blood tests and a chest X-ray taken that morning, along with an assessment of his condition. They have not yet disclosed the size of the tumor or whether the disease had spread to nearby lymph nodes, two key factors in determining prognosis.

Before returning to the Vatican, the pope celebrated Mass at the hospital in Rome. It was the second consecutive day he celebrated Mass there. The pope had announced last Sunday to the faithful gathered in St. Peter's Square that doctors had identified a nodule on his lung and that it would be removed.

On Sunday, Pope Leo will lead the Angelus prayer from the window of the papal apartment, according to the Vatican press office. It will be his first public engagement since the surgery, as he leads weekly prayers with pilgrims in St. Peter's Square at noon.
