Broncos beat Chiefs 28-24 after late false start wipes out winning touchdown

Denver stopped Kansas City at the goal line in the final seconds, after a false start wiped out a late Chiefs touchdown, to win 28-24 and move into first place in the AFC West.

2 November 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: ESPN, NBC Sports

The Denver Broncos beat the Kansas City Chiefs 28-24 on Sunday, 1 November, at Empower Field at Mile High in Denver. The game was decided in the final seconds, when Denver stopped Kansas City at the Denver goal line after a late penalty had wiped out what looked like the winning touchdown.

Denver had trailed 24-13 in the fourth quarter. The Broncos then scored two touchdowns in 4 minutes and 53 seconds of possession time to take a 28-24 lead with 1 minute and 56 seconds left. Jonah Coleman broke free on a 51-yard catch and run before scoring on a 5-yard run for the go-ahead touchdown.

Kansas City answered quickly. Emmett Johnson returned the kickoff 44 yards to the Kansas City 44, and Patrick Mahomes found Travis Kelce for a 32-yard gain. The Chiefs reached the Denver 4-yard line with 35 seconds left. Kenneth Walker ran for 3 yards to the 1, Mahomes threw incomplete on second down, and backup safety JL Skinner stopped Walker for no gain on third down.

On fourth and goal from the 1, Mahomes threw a pass that Rashee Rice caught in the end zone, but the play was blown dead because Chiefs right tackle Jaylon Moore was flagged for a false start. Mahomes said the call did not surprise him, but added that in big moments the small things are not usually called that way. Chiefs coach Andy Reid said he saw Moore move his head but did not see a false start. The penalty was one of 11 for 105 yards against Kansas City.

The penalty moved Kansas City back to the Denver 6 with four seconds left. Mahomes was pressured by Jonathon Cooper and sacked by Malcolm Roach as he tried to escape, ending the game. The win moved Denver to 6-2 and into first place in the AFC West, and it was the Broncos' 15th straight win in a one-score game. Kansas City fell to 5-2 after its second straight loss. Mahomes finished 30 of 41 for 321 yards and three touchdowns, while Denver quarterback Bo Nix finished 20 of 32 for 222 yards and two touchdowns.
