Trump calls LeBron James 'a total disgrace' for stretching during the national anthem

Trump accused LeBron James of disrespecting the US after the 76ers star kept warming up while the national anthem played at Madison Square Garden. James brushed it off.

24 October 2026 | US | By WPS Staff

Sources: USA Today, Al Jazeera

President Donald Trump said on Oct. 22 that NBA star LeBron James should apologize after accusing him of disrespecting the United States. Trump posted a video clip on Truth Social showing James moving through warm-up motions while the national anthem was sung before the Oct. 20 season opener between the Philadelphia 76ers and the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden.

Read More

"While listening to our National Anthem, LeBron James disrespects our Country!" Trump wrote. "I said right from the beginning that he's no good, a TOTAL DISGRACE. LeBron James should apologize to all of those Great American Warriors and Heroes who died for the American Flag, and all that our Country stands for!" The clip showed James swinging his arms like a golf swing and mimicking a jump shot while his teammates stood still with their heads down. The video carried the caption "LeBron working on his golf swing pregame."

James, 41, is playing a record 24th NBA season and his first with Philadelphia after eight seasons with the Los Angeles Lakers. He brushed off the attack after the 76ers' 114-106 comeback home win over the Cleveland Cavaliers on Thursday night. "It must be a slow news day at the office," he said. In the Oct. 20 opener at Madison Square Garden, Philadelphia beat the Knicks 106-100, with James scoring 14 points, nine rebounds and seven assists.

The two men have traded criticism for years. In 2018, James said on CNN that Trump was "using sports to kinda divide us." Trump fired back on what was then Twitter that James had made the host, Don Lemon, look smart despite Lemon being, in Trump's words, "the dumbest man on television." In July, speaking in the Oval Office, Trump suggested James could be "racist" and said Michael Jordan was the better player.

Trump has repeatedly criticized athletes over anthem behavior. He attacked NFL players in his first term for kneeling during the anthem to protest racial inequality and police brutality, and he canceled the Philadelphia Eagles' planned White House visit in 2018 after the team disagreed with his call for players to stand. James became a leading voice in the Black Lives Matter movement in 2020. A 76ers representative did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
