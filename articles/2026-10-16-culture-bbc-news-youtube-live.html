BBC News channel now streams live on YouTube in the UK and US

The BBC News channel is now available to stream live on YouTube in the UK and US, in a trial that started on 15 October.

16 October 2026 | Culture | By WPS Staff

Sources: Advanced Television, YouTube Official Blog

The BBC has announced that the BBC News channel is now available to stream live on YouTube in both the UK and US.

Read More

A trial, in immediate effect, provides viewers with coverage of international and domestic news, political developments, business, technology, culture and breaking events, supported by BBC News' global network of journalists and correspondents.

The launch is part of the BBC's wider YouTube strategy to reach new and younger audiences. The UK and US join a growing list of countries where the BBC News channel is available on YouTube, including Australia, Kenya, Nigeria and Poland.

Anjali Kapoor, Director of AI, Innovation and Growth for BBC News, said audiences increasingly expect news to be available wherever they are, on the platforms they use throughout their day. She said launching the BBC News channel on YouTube in the UK and US is an important step in bringing trusted journalism to even more people, and that it will make it easier for audiences to access live coverage of the stories shaping the world.

Alison Lomax, YouTube UK Managing Director, said it is an incredible milestone that YouTube's audiences in the UK, US, Australia, Kenya, Nigeria and Poland can now access the BBC's live news channel, making trusted news even more accessible to a new generation of digital first viewers. The BBC News channel is also available on BBC iPlayer, the BBC News website and app in the UK, and on cable, FAST and connected TV platforms, the BBC website and the BBC app in the US.
