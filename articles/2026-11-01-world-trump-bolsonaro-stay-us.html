Trump says Bolsonaro can stay in US as long as he wants

President Donald Trump said defeated Brazilian candidate Flavio Bolsonaro can stay in the United States as long as he wants, while Secretary of State Marco Rubio denied the US helped him flee Brazil.

1 November 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: Al Jazeera, Free Malaysia Today

President Donald Trump said on Saturday that defeated Brazilian presidential candidate Flavio Bolsonaro can stay in the United States as long as he wants. Trump spoke to reporters at his Florida residence, days after Bolsonaro lost the October 25 runoff election to President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and arrived in Miami on Wednesday.

Read More

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Saturday that the Trump administration did not help Bolsonaro leave Brazil. Speaking in Atlanta, where diplomats from the Group of 20 largest economies were gathered, Rubio said the United States did not facilitate anything, Bolsonaro had previously held a visa, and the president made a decision to allow him to enter the country.

Rubio added that the US government was not involved directly in any of the moves that led to Bolsonaro leaving Brazil earlier in the week. Some US media outlets have reported that White House official Jason Miller helped arrange Bolsonaro's journey. Rubio said he had seen Brazil's foreign minister, Mauro Vieira, in Atlanta on Friday and Saturday, and expected to see Brazilian officials again in France in November.

Bolsonaro travelled to the United States via Argentina and Ecuador after a Brazilian Supreme Court justice revoked his passport, as police considered him a flight risk. He faces investigation in Brazil over alleged corruption, money laundering and tax evasion, and Reuters has reported that he was admitted to the United States under humanitarian parole, a status that typically lasts for one year.

Bolsonaro has refused to concede the election and has called the result fraudulent without providing evidence, while outside observers have described the election as free and fair. Trump said he has no plans to meet Bolsonaro or support his political ambitions, called the election a disgrace, and said Bolsonaro can stay in the United States indefinitely, a position that may keep relations between the two countries strained.
