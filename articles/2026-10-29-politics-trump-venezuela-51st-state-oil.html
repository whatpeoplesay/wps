Trump says Venezuela could become the 51st US state

Speaking at a midterm rally in Corpus Christi, Texas, Donald Trump said Venezuela could elect a governor instead of a president, as he celebrated the arrival of 1 million barrels of Venezuelan oil.

29 October 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: ANews, Times Now

US President Donald Trump said Venezuela could become the 51st state of the United States, speaking at a midterm election rally in Corpus Christi, Texas, on 27 October. He said the country could one day be prosperous, and suggested it could elect a governor instead of a president.

Read More

Trump tied the idea to oil. He announced that the first 1 million barrels of Venezuelan crude oil under an agreement between the two countries had arrived in the United States, and he called the shipment a gift. He said the oil would be refined in Texas.

He repeated the idea during the speech, asking the crowd about making Venezuela the 51st state and electing a governor. He also turned to oil businessman Harold Hamm, who was in the room, and asked whether he liked the idea. Hamm said he did, according to the reports. Trump also thanked Venezuela for what he called a very, very good relationship.

The idea is not new. Earlier this year Trump said he was seriously considering making Venezuela a state, and he has made similar remarks about Canada. Venezuela has rejected the idea: acting President Delcy Rodriguez said in May that her country has no plans to become a US state, and that it would keep defending its integrity, sovereignty, independence and history.

Trump said the deal gave the United States control over a massive part of the world oil supply. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio travelled with Trump as national security adviser, according to Times Now.
