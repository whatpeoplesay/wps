Visitors told to leave Hatteras Island as coastal storm threatens Highway 12

Dare County ordered every visitor off Hatteras Island by 8 p.m. Friday, October 16, as a coastal storm threatened to flood and close N.C. Highway 12, the island's only road link.

17 October 2026 | US | By WPS Staff

Sources: WAVY, The Watchers

Dare County told every visitor on Hatteras Island to leave by 8 p.m. on Friday, October 16, after declaring a state of emergency. A coastal storm was heading for the Outer Banks, and officials wanted visitors off the island before the only road in and out became unusable. The order covers the island's seven villages.

The order applies to visitors only. Residents were not told to leave, and it does not cover areas north of Oregon Inlet. County officials said N.C. Highway 12 could stay shut for a long stretch if flood water and waves washing over the road keep coming.

The National Weather Service put a Coastal Flood Warning in place from Saturday afternoon, October 17, through Tuesday morning, October 20. Forecasters expected 2 to 4 feet of water above ground in low areas, breaking waves of 8 to 14 feet, and strong north winds with gusts up to 50 miles per hour. The worst overwash was expected around high tide at about 1 p.m. on Saturday.

Road crews worked to protect the highway before the storm arrived. They strengthened dunes along N.C. Highway 12 on Pea, Hatteras and Ocracoke islands, improved drainage, and placed equipment in weak spots. Ferry workers readied boats and gear at Rodanthe and Stumpy Point in case the emergency ferry route is needed again. Short closures of the highway and other exposed roads were possible.

The island is still recovering from a late September storm that tore up about 1,000 feet of the highway on Pea Island. That storm forced the county to run the Rodanthe to Stumpy Point emergency ferry for the first time since 2013. The damaged stretch reopened on September 29 as a one lane gravel road while longer repairs continue. Visitors were told to check road and ferry updates before traveling.
