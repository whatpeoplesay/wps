China proposes export controls on AI, biotech and solar technology

China proposed adding 40 technologies to its export control list. The plan covers artificial intelligence, biomedicine and solar power technology, with some exports banned and others limited.

16 October 2026 | Technology | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, The Hindu

China has proposed adding 40 technologies to its list of technologies that cannot be exported freely. The list covers artificial intelligence, biomedicine and solar power technology. The commerce ministry announced the plan on Friday.

Read More

The proposal covers AI models, intelligent agents and computing clusters. The ministry described these as the three basic building blocks of China's AI industry. Public comments are open until November 15, and China is also starting interim pre-export registration for the listed technologies.

Of the 40 technologies, 14 would face a full ban on export. These include technology for making perovskite and tandem solar-cell modules, AI technology that can create and change harmful code, and some platforms for cell and gene therapy. The other 26 would face limits on export.

The limits include engineering technology for polysilicon, which is used to make solar cells, when it involves production lines with yearly output of at least 30,000 tonnes. The ministry said the move was routine and did not target any specific country, region or company.

The ministry said uses of these technologies in sensitive fields keep growing. It said other major economies also apply export controls in these areas, and that China wants to keep normal international technology cooperation while lowering the risk of misuse.
