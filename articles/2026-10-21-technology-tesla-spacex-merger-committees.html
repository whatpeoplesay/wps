Musk confirms Tesla-SpaceX merger talks

Elon Musk said both companies formed special committees of independent directors to review a possible merger, but warned that a deal is far from certain.

21 October 2026 | Technology | By WPS Staff

Sources: Wall Street Journal, TechCrunch

Elon Musk said on Tuesday that the boards of Tesla and SpaceX have each set up a special committee to look at a possible merger of the two companies. He said the committees are made up of independent directors and were formed because shareholders asked for it.

Read More

Musk was speaking on SpaceX's third-quarter earnings call. He said he cannot take part in the talks because he leads both companies and is the largest individual shareholder in each, which makes him a conflicted party. He called the setup an odd kabuki dance, with two groups talking while he cannot join the conversation.

Musk said he supports the idea and sees a lot of merit in combining the companies, but he warned that a deal is not certain. He said the committees have not agreed on any terms, and he pushed back on the idea that a merger is a done deal.

The news moved the stocks. SpaceX shares rose more than 5 percent in after-hours trading after the call, and Tesla shares gained about 1 percent. The talk comes after a strong quarter for SpaceX, which posted its first profit as a public company, and after Musk merged his AI startup xAI into SpaceX earlier this year.

A combination would put the world's biggest commercial rocket launcher and satellite internet operator together with a major electric car maker under one roof. Analysts have been talking about the idea for weeks. A Barclays analyst said a deal could be announced before Tesla's annual meeting in December, but Musk said it is too early to assume anything.
