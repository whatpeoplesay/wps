Technical fault disrupts air traffic across Italy, ENAV says

Air travellers across Italy faced delays and cancellations on Friday after a technical fault affected air traffic control. Officials said the fault was not caused by malicious activity.

16 October 2026 | Travel | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, Travel And Tour World

Air travellers faced delays and cancellations across Italy on Friday because of a technical fault affecting air traffic control. Authorities said the fault was not caused by malicious activity.

Read More

Italy air traffic authority ENAV said it introduced temporary restrictions on air traffic capacity. The move came after problems were found overnight on the data transmission network that supports air traffic control services across the country.

Rome airports operator ADR said flight delays and cancellations may occur because of technical issues affecting air traffic control systems nationwide. It advised passengers to contact their airline.

Milan airports company SEA issued a similar notice to passengers. It also warned of possible delays and cancellations and told travellers to check with their airline.

ENAV said the source of the problem has been identified. It said efforts are under way to restore full operations.
