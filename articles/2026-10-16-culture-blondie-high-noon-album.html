Blondie announce new album High Noon for February 2027

Blondie have announced High Noon, their 12th studio album and first new music in nine years. It will be released on 26 February 2027 via BMG.

16 October 2026 | Culture | By WPS Staff

Sources: Gold Radio, Louder Than War

Blondie have announced their new studio album High Noon. The band's 12th album and follow-up to 2017's Pollinator is released on February 26, 2027 via BMG and is available to pre-order in various formats.

Read More

Lead single and opening track Spectral Eyes, written with and featuring ex-Smiths guitarist Johnny Marr, is available to stream now.

As well as featuring contributions from guest stars like Johnny Marr, Sex Pistols' Glen Matlock, Zach Condon of Beirut, and Jess Wolfe and Holly Laessig of Lucius, the album boasts drumming from band ever-present Clem Burke, who died last year at the age of 70.

Frontwoman Debbie Harry said the album is classic Blondie, an amalgamation of all their different influences and eras into something new. She said the music feels grounded in the present, shaped by who they are and how they are living now. Chris Stein said it has been a long, strange ride, and that the band are incredibly grateful for all the support over the years.

The full track list has 13 songs, from Spectral Eyes to Ah, Sunflower, Weary of Time. As for whether Blondie will ever take High Noon on the road, the matter is still very much up in the air, with Burke's death and Chris Stein's absence from the touring lineup for health reasons. Harry said last year that she could not see herself being onstage as Blondie without Burke and without Stein onstage, but that she is proud of the music, and would still like to do music.
