Trump will respect midterms only if honest elections

Donald Trump said on October 27 he will respect the November 3 midterm results only if they are honest elections, and said he would use his veto and find a way to work with Democrats if they took control of Congress.

28 October 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: USA Today, CNA

US President Donald Trump said on October 27 that he will respect the results of the November 3 midterm elections only if they are honest elections. He was asked by reporters whether he would respect the results regardless of the outcome, and he answered, "I will, if they're honest elections."

Read More

Trump was speaking as he headed on a campaign trip to Texas, after being asked whether he would accept the results even if his Republican Party lost control of Congress. He gave a guarded pledge to work with Democrats if they took over, saying, "We'll figure a way. Look, I have strong veto. I hope that that won't be the case, but if it is, that's happened before with many presidents."

His comments come as polling points to a possible Democratic blue wave, with Democrats widely favoured to win control of the House of Representatives and having several paths to take control of the Senate. Trump has held near-daily rallies across the United States over the past month to increase turnout among his supporters.

This week Trump also announced a $1 million payment for anyone who reports voter fraud in past elections or the midterms, if the tip leads to an arrest and conviction. He has in recent weeks regularly accused Democrats of cheating to win elections, without presenting evidence, according to USA Today, while CNA describes his claims of mass voter fraud as baseless and years old.

Some critics fear Trump is paving the way to challenge the midterm results, or even bring in emergency powers, CNA reported. Trump has not ruled out declaring a national emergency around the midterms, USA Today reported, and he has taken steps this year aimed at restricting voting, including an order on mail ballots that the Supreme Court intervened to stop for this election.
