Brewers beat Dodgers 6-4 to sweep NLCS and reach first World Series since 1982

Milwaukee came back from 4-0 down to win Game 4 in Los Angeles. The sweep sends the Brewers to the World Series for the first time since 1982.

16 October 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: NBC Sports, Reuters

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Los Angeles Dodgers 6-4 on Thursday in Game 4 of the National League Championship Series. The win completed a four-game sweep and sent Milwaukee to the World Series for the first time since 1982, against the two-time defending champions.

Read More

The Dodgers led 4-0 in the second inning after Shohei Ohtani hit his first home run of the postseason, a three-run shot. Andy Pages had driven in the first run of the inning with a single. The crowd of 49,104 at Dodger Stadium celebrated the early lead.

Milwaukee started its comeback in the fourth inning. Jake Bauers hit a ground-rule double that cut the lead to 4-2. In the fifth, the Brewers tied the game at 4-4. David Hamilton scored on a single, and Bauers walked with the bases loaded to force in the tying run.

Garrett Mitchell put Milwaukee ahead for the first time in the sixth inning. After falling behind 0-2, he hit a 409-foot home run to center field. It was his second home run of the series, after he also homered in the 6-2 win on Wednesday.

Milwaukee's bullpen held the lead the rest of the way. David Hamilton hit a two-out solo home run in the ninth inning for a 6-4 cushion. The Brewers are the first major league club to sweep a team in the postseason one year after being swept by that same team. Milwaukee lost its only other World Series appearance, to the St. Louis Cardinals in seven games in 1982.
