Song Joong-ki Frowns After Fan Mentions Ex-Wife in China

Song Joong-ki was greeting fans on a street in China when a fan mentioned his ex-wife Song Hye-kyo. His smile faded for a moment, and the clip went viral.

23 October 2026 | Culture | By WPS Staff

Sources: Financial News Korea, MK (Maeil Business Newspaper)

A short fan video taken on a street in China this week caught Song Joong-ki in an awkward moment. The South Korean actor was greeting fans, dressed casually in a cap, sweatshirt and jeans, smiling and waving. Someone in the crowd asked who he was, and another voice answered with the name of his ex-wife, Song Hye-kyo. His smile faded for a moment.

Read More

The change in his face was caught clearly on camera. He looked uncomfortable for a few seconds, then relaxed, smiled again, and kept greeting the local fans around him. The clip started moving fast across social media the same day.

Online, the video split people into two camps. Many called the remark rude and said his reaction was a normal human one. Others argued that a public figure should expect such moments, and some defended the fan as careless but not cruel. The comment sections filled up fast.

The two actors met on the 2016 drama Descendants of the Sun, married in October 2017, and divorced in July 2019. Both have continued acting since then.

Song Joong-ki remarried British actress Katy Louise Saunders in 2023, and the couple has a son and a daughter. The video ended with him still smiling and greeting the crowd.
