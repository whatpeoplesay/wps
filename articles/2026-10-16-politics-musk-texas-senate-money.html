Musk gave more than $150 million to back the GOP in the Texas Senate race, filings show

New campaign finance filings show Elon Musk gave $75 million and $83 million to the Senate Leadership Fund in September. The money then moved to a Texas group backing Ken Paxton.

16 October 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: CNN, Business Standard (Bloomberg)

New filings with the Federal Election Commission on Thursday shed light on tech billionaire Elon Musk's heavy influence on the 2026 midterm elections, above all in the Senate race in Texas. According to the third quarter report from Senate Leadership Fund, the main Republican super PAC focused on Senate races, Musk donated $75 million on September 9 and another $83 million on September 23. The two donations add up to more than $150 million. Musk had already given more than a quarter of a billion dollars to support President Donald Trump's 2024 presidential campaign.

The filing shows that the Senate Leadership Fund transferred $75 million to Texas PAC on September 10, one day after Musk's first donation, and another $83 million on September 24, one day after his second donation. Texas PAC was formed for the unusually competitive Senate race in Texas. The Republican-aligned groups have backed Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's bid against Democrat James Talarico in the race for the seat now held by Republican Senator John Cornyn.

Musk has built a large business presence in Texas, centering operations there for SpaceX, Tesla, and X, among his companies. In 2024, he moved the headquarters of X and SpaceX to Texas from California. In September, as Musk's money began flowing to Republicans, GOP ad support jumped. Texas PAC spent more than $77 million on ads in that month alone, according to AdImpact data.

In total, including the September surge and bookings through Election Day, Texas PAC is set to spend nearly $160 million on ads backing Paxton. The race is one of the most watched contests in the country, and two new polls find no clear leader, even as ad spending has passed half a billion dollars. On Wednesday night, Talarico took questions from Texas voters alone at a town hall hosted by CNN and Jubilee.

The filings came the same day Talarico's campaign announced it had raised a record $64 million in the third quarter of this year. That passed the previous quarterly record of $57.9 million set by former South Carolina Senate candidate Jaime Harrison in 2020. Talarico has now raised more than $134.3 million since launching his campaign in September 2025, with most of it coming from small contributions, his campaign said. Paxton raised $18 million in the third quarter through his campaign and affiliated committees. While Talarico may lead in fundraising, Republicans still hold a large spending advantage, and he trails in money spent on television.
