Malaysia Airlines resumes Jeddah and Madinah flights from October 18

Malaysia Airlines will fly to and from Jeddah and Madinah again from October 18, after a short suspension. The airline says the restart follows a full safety review.

18 October 2026 | Travel | By WPS Staff

Sources: Malay Mail, The Straits Times

Malaysia Airlines will resume scheduled flights to and from Jeddah and Madinah in Saudi Arabia from October 18. The airline announced the restart on October 17, after a temporary suspension of services to the two cities.

Read More

The airline said the decision followed a comprehensive safety review and an operational risk assessment. It said the safety and security of passengers and crew remained its highest priority, and that it would keep watching developments in the region.

Passengers travelling to or from Jeddah or Madinah are asked to check their flight schedules. They should also make sure their contact details are up to date through the My Booking feature on the airline website or mobile app.

Travellers who booked through third-party platforms or travel agents should contact their booking provider directly about their travel plans. Customers who need help can use the Live Chat feature on the airline website or call the Global Contact Centre, on 1 300 88 3000 inside Malaysia or +603 7843 3000 from abroad.

The airline thanked passengers for their patience and understanding during the temporary suspension. It said services would restart as scheduled from October 18.
