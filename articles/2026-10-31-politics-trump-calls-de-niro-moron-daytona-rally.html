Trump calls De Niro a 'moron' at Daytona rally as Hollywood seeks tax credits

President Donald Trump called actor Robert De Niro a moron at a Daytona Beach rally for Byron Donalds, then mocked Hollywood figures seeking film and television tax credits.

31 October 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: New York Post, TheWrap

President Donald Trump used a campaign rally in Daytona Beach, Florida, on Friday to attack actor Robert De Niro, calling him a moron and saying there was something wrong with him. The rally was held for Republican gubernatorial candidate Byron Donalds, days before the November 3 midterm elections. Trump asked the crowd whether De Niro was a moron, then answered his own question, and went on to call the actor gross and terrible.

Trump also said De Niro was no good for comedies and suggested someone should tell him so. While talking about his former television show, he said it had beaten what used to be a great show, the Academy Awards, and complained that the awards show now suffers because people get up and use foul language about him. TheWrap reported that representatives for De Niro and the Academy did not immediately respond to its request for comment.

The attack on De Niro came as Trump talked about Hollywood figures seeking tax credits for film and television production. He said people in Hollywood who had criticised him for years were now calling him and asking for meetings, and he claimed they wanted tax credits because Hollywood was dying and other countries were taking jobs away. He said he would probably approve the credits, although he gave no details of any plan.

Trump described the meetings in mocking terms, saying visitors who had spoken badly about him for years were now sitting in his office and calling it a great honour to meet him. He said the internet made it easy to check everything a person had said over the past 20 years, and he acted out a visitor claiming to have been misquoted after calling him a bad name.

The two men have clashed in public before. The New York Post noted De Niro's speech at the 2018 Tony Awards, and reported that the actor has been raising money for Democrats ahead of Tuesday's elections. De Niro also spoke at a voter mobilisation event in New York on October 5, according to the Post.
