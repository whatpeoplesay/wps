Sydney Airport security staff walk off twice in one day

Travellers were warned to expect delays at Sydney Airport as hundreds of security workers walked off the job for a second time in one day. Staff stopped work in the morning and again in the evening.

16 October 2026 | Travel | By WPS Staff

Sources: AAP, Travel Monitor

Travellers were told to expect delays at Sydney Airport as hundreds of security workers walked off the job for a second time in one day. Staff at the airport domestic and international terminals held a second four-hour stoppage on Friday evening, after walking off in the morning.

Read More

The workers are contracted by the airport from security firm Certis Group. They are demanding more full time jobs, fairer rostering and no cuts to hours.

Transport Workers Union National Secretary Michael Kaine said the decision to strike came after unsuccessful bargaining talks with the company on Thursday. He said the company had pressed forward with plans to cut minimum hours by six hours per week. The union also said job insecurity and limited pay have left some security staff fatigued and forced to work multiple jobs.

Certis Group said it remained committed to negotiating in good faith and had put a fresh offer to staff as recently as Thursday. A spokesperson said the company was disappointed that the union had chosen to proceed with industrial action that affects the travelling public.

Security staff were expected to strike from 6am to 10am and from 5pm to 9pm. Sydney Airport said it was working with its partners to minimise disruption and maintain passenger safety, and it advised passengers travelling on Friday to allow extra time and stay alert for messages from their airline. The action followed walk-offs at the airport in late September involving a reported 400 staff.
