Woman sobs for three hours on 10-hour flight after seatmate refuses seat swap

A travel blogger refused to give up her aisle seat on an Istanbul to Washington DC flight, and the stranger in the middle seat cried for three hours. Online, the debate is split between kindness and booking your own seats.

20 October 2026 | Travel | By WPS Staff

Sources: New York Post, Creatorzine

A woman cried for about three hours on a ten-hour flight after a fellow passenger refused to give up her aisle seat so the woman could sit with her husband.

Read More

Travel blogger Tomiko Harvey, 55, from Richmond, Virginia, was flying home from Istanbul to Washington DC on October 9 after a trip to Kazakhstan. She had booked the aisle seat for the long flight. A woman in the middle seat of her row asked Harvey to trade places so she could sit beside her husband. Harvey politely said no.

The tears then started and did not stop for about three hours. Harvey finally asked the woman if someone had died, because who cries for three hours. The woman said nobody had died. She just missed her husband and wanted to rest her head on his shoulder. Her husband, it turned out, was two rows away in a middle seat, with his headphones on, sleeping through the whole flight.

When the plane landed, Harvey congratulated the woman for surviving ten hours without her husband. She joked that she now needed to reevaluate her own marriage, saying she clearly did not love her husband enough. Harvey shared footage of the flight online, with the woman's face blurred, and it picked up more than 90,000 likes.

She asked her followers a simple question: what is wrong with people who ask a stranger to switch seats on a long flight. If you want to sit together, she said, book together or pay for seat selection. Most commenters sided with her, saying couples should arrange their seats in advance instead of expecting others to move. A few said she could have been nicer to her seatmate.
