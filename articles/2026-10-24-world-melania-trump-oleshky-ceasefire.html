Melania Trump brokers Oleshky ceasefire in Ukraine

The White House says first lady Melania Trump brokered a localized ceasefire in the Russian-occupied town of Oleshky. Both sides agreed to stop fighting so aid can get in and civilians can leave.

24 October 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, USA Today

The White House announced on Oct. 23 that first lady Melania Trump brokered a localized ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine around the town of Oleshky in southern Ukraine. Both sides agreed to temporarily halt military operations so humanitarian aid can enter the town and civilians can be evacuated.

The pause is set to begin in the coming days. Russia's top military officer, Valery Gerasimov, said Russian troops would halt fire at 4 a.m. local time on Oct. 27. The first lady said the humanitarian pause would start Oct. 29. The operation will cover food deliveries, clearing roads of mines, and moving civilians through safe corridors.

Oleshky sits across the Dnipro River from the Ukrainian-held city of Kherson and has been occupied by Russian forces since the start of the full-scale invasion. About 1,700 to 2,000 people remain there, down from 24,000 before the war, and most are elderly. Residents have reported a deepening food crisis, and the town has been largely cut off from food and power.

The first lady said she became aware of the crisis after a private discussion during the U.N. General Assembly in New York last month. Ukraine had also asked other world leaders for help, and Pope Leo XIV raised the town's situation with Russian President Vladimir Putin in a phone call on Oct. 16.

The deal came with wider moves toward talks. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said negotiators from Ukraine, Russia and the United States will hold technical-level meetings in Abu Dhabi in the first half of November, and he hoped a leaders' meeting would follow. On the same day, the two sides swapped 270 prisoners each, the first exchange since August.
