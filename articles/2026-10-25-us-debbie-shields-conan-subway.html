NYC bar owner says 89 seconds with Conan O'Brien changed her life

Debbie Shields went viral after a short subway exchange with Conan O'Brien, and says the 89 seconds changed her life, brought new customers to her Brooklyn bar, and left her thinking about stand-up.

25 October 2026 | US | By WPS Staff

Sources: New York Post, Bored Panda

A Brooklyn bar owner says a chance meeting on a New York subway train has changed her life, after a short improvised exchange with comedian Conan O'Brien was watched more than a million times online.

Read More

Debbie Shields, who runs Confessions Bar in Bedford-Stuyvesant, was travelling on the F train when she saw O'Brien filming an episode of the online series SubwayTakes with host Kareem Rahma, and she decided to walk over and sit down with them.

The two quickly built a made-up story together, joking that they had been married long ago and had two sons, one in medical school and the other in jail, and Shields kept the jokes going when O'Brien apologised for never sending money, telling him his pictures were all over the house.

O'Brien called the short meeting the best year and a half of his life, and Shields answered that she had heard the same line from her other three husbands, before telling the host that she had thrown O'Brien out of her bar twice and offering him two free drinks as the father of her fictional children.

Since the clip spread, Shields says strangers now recognise her on the street, her bar has gained thousands of new followers online and added a drink called The Baby Daddy O'Brien, and she is now thinking about trying stand-up comedy herself.
