The Chicks postpone Los Angeles show hours before showtime, fans demand a cancellation

The Chicks postponed their November 2 show at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles hours before it was due to start, citing illness, and moved it to November 4, leaving many fans angry about the late notice and refunds.

4 November 2026 | Culture | By WPS Staff

Sources: USA Today, New York Post

The Chicks have postponed their November 2 concert at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles just hours before it was due to start, citing illness. The country trio, made up of Natalie Maines, Emily Strayer and Martie Maguire, announced the change on Instagram, and the show was rescheduled for Wednesday, November 4.

In their statement, the group said all tickets bought for November 2 would be honoured on the new date. Fans who could not attend on November 4 were told refunds would be available at their point of purchase, and the group apologised for any inconvenience.

Many fans criticised how late the announcement came, saying they were told about ninety minutes, or about an hour and a half, before the doors were due to open. Some had already travelled to Los Angeles and paid for hotels, while others said they only found out after arriving at the venue or reaching the parking garage.

The biggest complaint was about refunds. Because the show was rescheduled rather than cancelled, fans who bought tickets through resale sites such as StubHub, SeatGeek and Gametime said they could not get automatic refunds. One fan said they stood to lose over 1,200 dollars, and others said they had already paid for flights and hotels they could not recover.

The rescheduled show on November 4 is set to be the final North American stop of the group's Taking the Long Way 20th Anniversary tour, which honours their Grammy-winning album of the same name. The tour began on September 30 at the Fox Theatre in Detroit and included major stops across the United States and Canada.
