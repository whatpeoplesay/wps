India recall Bhuvneshwar Kumar for West Indies T20 decider in Bengaluru

Bhuvneshwar Kumar returns to India's T20I squad for the series decider against West Indies, as Arshdeep Singh recovers from a shoulder injury.

16 October 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, Indian Express

India have added fast bowler Bhuvneshwar Kumar to their squad for the fifth and deciding Twenty20 international against West Indies. The board announced the move on Friday.

Read More

Bhuvneshwar comes in as cover for Arshdeep Singh, who is recovering from a shoulder injury. Arshdeep hurt his bowling shoulder during the fourth T20I in Hyderabad and retired hurt after being struck while batting.

West Indies won the fourth match to level the five-match series at 2-2. The deciding match will be played in Bengaluru on Saturday.

A decision on Arshdeep's availability for the decider will be taken before the match, based on his recovery.

Bhuvneshwar, 36, returns to India's T20I setup for the first time in nearly four years. He has also been named in India's squad for the T20I series in New Zealand, which begins on October 22.
