Mahela Jayawardene steps down as Mumbai Indians head coach

Mumbai Indians have announced that Mahela Jayawardene has stepped down as head coach, ending a decade-long association that brought three IPL titles.

17 October 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: The Indian Express, The Hindu

Mahela Jayawardene has stepped down as head coach of Mumbai Indians, the franchise announced on Saturday. His departure ends a second stint of two seasons in charge and closes a decade-long association with the five-time IPL champions. Mumbai Indians will now look for a new head coach ahead of the next season.

Read More

Jayawardene first took charge of Mumbai Indians in 2017 and stayed in the role through 2022. In that first spell he led the team to three IPL titles, in 2017, 2019 and 2020. He then moved into the role of Global Head of Performance as the franchise grew its presence in leagues around the world.

He returned as head coach ahead of the 2025 season, replacing Mark Boucher. Mumbai reached the playoffs in 2025, but the 2026 season was a difficult one and the team struggled to stay competitive. The franchise said it wanted a fresh outlook after the underwhelming campaign.

In his farewell message, Jayawardene said Mumbai Indians had been a very special part of his journey for over a decade. He thanked the players, the coaching team and the fans, and thanked Nita Ambani and Akash Ambani for their trust and support. He said he left with immense pride in what the team had achieved together and wished the team success in the years ahead.

Franchise owner Nita Ambani thanked Jayawardene for his calm leadership, integrity and deep love for the game. She said he would always be a part of the MI family. Mumbai Indians have started looking for a successor ahead of the mid-November retention deadline, with the new coach set to lead the team into the IPL 2027 season.
