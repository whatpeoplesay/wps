China Airlines raises extra luggage and pet travel fees

China Airlines will raise its fees for extra luggage and the transport of pets from October 16. The increases will amount to between 10 percent and 30 percent.

16 October 2026 | Travel | By WPS Staff

Sources: Taiwan News, Travel And Tour World

China Airlines will raise its fees for extra luggage and the transport of pets from October 16. The increases will amount to between 10 percent and 30 percent. Taiwan's main carrier already raised fees for the same services on January 1.

Read More

The airline said the new price adjustments are the result of general market conditions. For most destinations in East Asia as well as Palau and Guam, fees for extra luggage will increase to around NT$5,100 (US$160) from NT$4,470.

The largest increases will be applied for destinations in North America, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, and India. The hike for extra luggage will amount to NT$1,270, or 20 percent.

Prepaid excess baggage will become between 20 percent and 30 percent more expensive, reaching NT$4,850 for East Asia, Palau, and Guam. The transportation of pets under 32 kilograms will cost NT$13,000 per one-way flight in Asia, instead of the previous NT$9,000. The ticket for Europe and North America will cost NT$19,000.

Competitors EVA Air and Starlux Airlines introduced new rates for similar services in September. Travellers should check the charges that apply to their own route and ticket before they travel.
