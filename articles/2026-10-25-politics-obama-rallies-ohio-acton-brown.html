Obama rallies Ohio crowd for Acton and Brown

Obama joined Acton and Brown in Cleveland, calling Ramaswamy an opportunist and Husted a rubber stamp, as Ohio's two tight races headed into their final days.

25 October 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: USA Today, AsiaOne

Former President Barack Obama campaigned in Cleveland on Oct. 24 alongside Democratic governor candidate Amy Acton and Senate candidate Sherrod Brown. The rally at Cleveland Public Auditorium drew thousands of supporters as Democrats tried to build energy for two tight Ohio races ahead of the Nov. 3 election.

Read More

Obama turned his fire on President Donald Trump, blaming him for rising costs and attacking his policies on immigration and foreign policy. He called Republican governor candidate Vivek Ramaswamy an opportunist and Sen. Jon Husted a rubber stamp for the Trump administration, and said voters were not stuck with what he called a spineless Congress and Republican governors enabling lawlessness and cruelty toward working people.

The visit was Obama's first campaign trip to Ohio since 2018. Brown told the crowd that the national media would never write off Ohio again, while a spokesperson for Husted said Obama and Brown backed policies that raised taxes and brought chaos at the southern border, and said seeing the two together should send shivers down Ohioans' spines.

Obama also condemned Ramaswamy's ad featuring Acton's estranged mother, saying standards of behaviour had fallen so low that someone could run such an ad and adding that no office was worth stooping that low. Ramaswamy has stood by the ad and rejected calls to take it down, while Acton told the crowd her opponent did not understand Ohio, was out of touch and out for himself, and was a danger to her state.

Both Ohio races remain close, with Brown's Senate contest able to help decide control of the U.S. Senate and Acton's governor race at the centre of the state's midterm map. With Nov. 3 approaching, Ohio has become one of the most watched battlegrounds in the country.
