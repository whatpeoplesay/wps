Iran Says 7 Oil Tankers Attacked in Strait of Hormuz as War Fears Grow

Iran says seven oil tankers caught fire or were damaged overnight in the Strait of Hormuz. The claim could not be independently confirmed, and fears of a return to war are growing.

23 October 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: Korea Times, DT Next

Seven oil tankers caught fire or were damaged in the Strait of Hormuz overnight, Tehran said on Thursday. Iranian state television reported the claim, citing the navy of the Revolutionary Guard Corps. The report did not mention any casualties, and the claims could not be independently confirmed.

Read More

The report said the tankers were hit by Iran or struck naval mines. The Guard's navy also threatened to strike American military bases in the region if the United States keeps helping guide commercial ships through the waterway.

The timing is tense. Israel is days away from a close election in which Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu could lose power, and the United States heads into midterm elections next month, where prices linked to the war have angered voters. There are fears that American and Israeli attacks on Iran could resume.

Iran's foreign minister, Abbas Araghchi, said on Wednesday that Tehran is still open to diplomacy but ready to defend itself. The United States is sending more military power to the region, including a third aircraft carrier and thousands of troops, to join the largest American force in the Middle East in decades. Mediators are meanwhile trying to bring both sides back to formal talks.

A return to full fighting would again put civilians across the region at risk and disrupt shipments of oil, natural gas and products like fertilizer, likely pushing prices up. A British military monitor said October has seen the most reported attacks on commercial shipping since March.
