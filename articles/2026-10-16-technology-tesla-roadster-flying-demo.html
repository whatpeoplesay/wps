Tesla shows new Roadster that lifts off the ground with thrusters

Tesla showed a new Roadster at a SpaceX site in Texas. The car lifted off the ground with thrusters, turned in the air, and landed back on its wheels.

16 October 2026 | Technology | By WPS Staff

Sources: New York Post, Deccan Chronicle

Tesla showed its new Roadster on Thursday at a SpaceX site in McGregor, Texas. The car lifted off the ground with thrusters, turned in the air, and landed using its built-in thrusters. No person was inside the car during the flight part of the show.

Read More

The car first drove upside down on a curved ramp. A fan suction system on the bottom held it to the ramp. It then rose to about 21 feet in the air, flew over a truck and other Tesla cars, turned, and landed. It also did a kick-flip move in a later part of the show.

Tesla said the car can go from 0 to 60 miles per hour in 0.81 seconds. It said the top speed can reach up to 230 miles per hour and the range is at least 300 miles. Elon Musk said the car might go even faster than 230 miles per hour.

The Roadster will be the only car Tesla makes that a person drives by hand, the company said. It has a steer-by-wire system built in. Musk said he was not sure the car would be legal in every place, but said there will be a car that someone can own.

Production is planned to start in 2028. A Performance version needs a 50,000 dollar deposit, and a Thrust version with acceleration thrusters needs a 250,000 dollar deposit. The new car comes 18 years after the first Roadster, Tesla's first electric car.
