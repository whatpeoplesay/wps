Poet, 70, arrested for slapping teen at polling place

Police in Laconia, New Hampshire, arrested 70-year-old poet Stephen Redic after video showed him slapping an 18-year-old Turning Point Action volunteer across the face outside a polling place on Election Day.

5 November 2026 | US | By WPS Staff

Sources: New York Post, Fox News

Police in Laconia, New Hampshire, arrested 70-year-old poet Stephen Redic on Wednesday morning, 4 November, after video showed him slapping an 18-year-old Turning Point Action volunteer across the face outside a polling place where people were voting on Tuesday. Officers took him into custody at about 8 a.m. on charges of simple assault, a misdemeanor, and disorderly conduct, a violation.

Police said Redic had approached the Turning Point tent as he was leaving the polling place at an elementary school in Laconia and wanted to read a poem about rich people. The video does not include the poem. It shows the young volunteer trying to debate him while Redic raises his voice and grows angrier, then points a finger in the volunteer's face.

In the video, Redic says, "You sit there and dare to look me in the face and say I don't know anything?" He then says, "That's what I know," and backhands the young man across the face. He gives him the middle finger, swears as he walks away, and people off camera talk about calling the police. Onlookers could be heard reacting in surprise.

Laconia Police Chief Matthew Canfield said police had no earlier contact with Redic. Turning Point described the volunteer as an 18-year-old. Redic published a book of poems in 2023 called "Tying My Shoes," in which he wrote that he had met every US president since Dwight D. Eisenhower. He is due in court on 10 December for an arraignment. It was not clear on Wednesday afternoon whether he had a lawyer, and he did not respond to an attempt to reach him.

The incident comes 14 months after Charlie Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University under a similar tent. Kirk founded Turning Point USA and its affiliate, Turning Point Action. His accused killer, Tyler Robinson, is due in court on Monday for a hearing that could set a trial date, and he has pleaded not guilty.
