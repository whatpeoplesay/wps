Europe backs Eli Lilly weight loss pill Foundayo for obesity and diabetes

A European medicines panel recommended approval of Eli Lilly's once-daily weight loss pill Foundayo for weight management and type 2 diabetes in adults, a step toward a second oral weight loss drug in the region.

16 October 2026 | Health | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, European Medicines Agency

The European Medicines Agency's committee on Friday recommended approval of Eli Lilly's weight loss pill Foundayo. The recommendation paves the way for a second oral weight loss drug to win backing in the region, after Novo Nordisk's Wegovy pill.

Read More

The committee recommended granting a marketing authorisation for Foundayo for weight management and treatment of type 2 diabetes in adults. Foundayo, also known by the name orforglipron, is an oral GLP-1 receptor agonist, and it will be available as a tablet taken once daily.

The European Commission will now review the recommendation and give a final nod before the company can launch the drug. Novo's Wegovy pill won EU approval in July, and the recommendation for Foundayo now goes through the same final step.

The agency said the once-daily tablet provides an alternative to injectable and other oral GLP-1 receptor agonists. Foundayo won US approval in April, becoming the second GLP-1 pill cleared for weight loss after Novo Nordisk's Wegovy pill, and Britain's medicines regulator approved it in August.

Foundayo is a prescription medicine. The regulator will keep its safety and effectiveness under close review, and health authorities have warned patients against buying GLP-1 medicines from unregulated sellers.
