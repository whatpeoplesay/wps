3 children and a teacher were killed in Oregon elementary school shooting, police say. The teen shooter is also dead

Police say a 16-year-old entered Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, and started shooting. Three children and a teacher were killed.

16 October 2026 | US | By WPS Staff

Source: CNN

Three children, aged 5, 8 and 11, and one teacher were killed when a 16-year-old resident of Bend, Oregon, entered an elementary school and started shooting, police said at a news conference on Thursday evening.

The shooter was armed with a long gun and forced his way into the school by shooting at locked doors, said Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz. It was not clear how far he got inside the building. A community member who saw the teenager entering Silver Rail Elementary School at about 1 p.m. followed him in and exchanged gunfire with him, killing him before officers arrived.

A 7-year-old child and another teacher were also injured and taken to a hospital. Police said it was not yet clear whether the shooter had any link to the school, what kind of long gun he used, how many rounds he fired, or what his motive was. Police did not name him at the news conference.

The first 911 call reported that an unknown man had shot into a car. Moments later, more calls reported that a person had entered the school and started shooting. The school was placed on lockdown, and police set up a reunification point for families. The FBI is on the scene and helping with the investigation.

Oregon Governor Tina Kotek called the community member heroic and said the person intervened to stop further loss of life. Bend-La Pine Schools Superintendent Steven Cook said crisis counsellors from across the region will be available to students, families and staff. All Bend-La Pine Schools will be closed on Friday. Bend Mayor Melanie Kebler said there will be gatherings in the coming days to mourn and to begin the process of healing.
