Texas Senate candidate James Talarico goes viral making masala dosa

Texas Senate candidate James Talarico drew online praise after a video showed him making a masala dosa at an Indian restaurant in Irving, days before voters head to the polls.

1 November 2026 | US | By WPS Staff

Sources: Indian Express, News18

A video of Texas Senate candidate James Talarico making a masala dosa has gone viral during his campaign. The video, published by WFAA, shows Talarico at Madira N Masala, an Indian restaurant in Irving, Texas, spreading dosa batter on a hot tava and then adding the potato filling.

Read More

Talarico made the dosa on his first try, and his proud face after finishing it caught the eye of viewers. A woman standing beside him quietly showed him how to get it right as others watched with interest.

The restaurant visit was part of his outreach to the Indian-American community. Immigration reform and celebrating diversity were among the issues he spoke about during the visit.

The video won over many viewers online, with users praising the candidate for taking an interest in the community he hopes to represent. One user said it was good to see a political leader who cares about the community, while others said the dish looked delicious.

Talarico, a Democrat, is in a closely watched race against Republican Ken Paxton, the Texas attorney general, with the midterm election set for November 3. A poll released on October 29 put Talarico ahead by two points, with 49 percent support against Paxton's 47 percent. Talarico was first elected to the Texas House of Representatives in 2018 and is serving his fourth term.
