Stephen Miller replaces Susie Wiles as White House chief of staff

President Donald Trump has named Stephen Miller as his new White House chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles, who will move to his political operation.

7 November 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: CNN, USA Today

President Donald Trump announced on Friday, November 6, that Stephen Miller will replace Susie Wiles as White House chief of staff. Miller will start in the job on Monday, November 9. Trump made the announcement in a post on Truth Social, calling Miller one of his most trusted advisers. Wiles will move over to the Trump Political Operation.

Wiles submitted her resignation on Thursday evening, according to two sources familiar with the matter. In a statement on X, Wiles said serving Trump was the honor of a lifetime and that she was proud of what they had accomplished together. She added that while her role may be changing, her commitment to the president and the mission never will.

The change is the first major staff shake-up since the midterm elections on Tuesday, November 3, when Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate. Trump had grown angry as he watched coverage of the historic losses, and he blamed his political team for the strategy behind the defeats. Allies had urged him to change his senior staff in response to the losses.

Miller has worked for Trump since his first presidential campaign began in 2015. As deputy chief of staff, he was the mastermind behind some of Trump’s most polarizing priorities, including the hardline immigration agenda, and over the past year he widened his portfolio to wide areas of domestic policy. USA Today described him as a top loyalist with little interest in working with Democrats in Congress.

Wiles was the first woman to serve as White House chief of staff, and she had been Trump’s campaign manager in the 2024 election. She lasted longer in the job than any of the four chiefs of staff Trump had in his first term. Trump had said as recently as October 16 that she would stay for a long time. Earlier this year Wiles was diagnosed with early-stage breast cancer, and in September she announced she was cancer-free after treatment.
