Clinton slams McConaughey over independent vote video

Hillary Clinton called Matthew McConaughey's video backing independent candidates "really unfortunate", saying voters should back Democrats with the midterms two weeks away.

23 October 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: New York Post, Fox News

Hillary Clinton pushed back hard against Matthew McConaughey this week over a video the actor posted urging voters to consider independent candidates. McConaughey's nearly six-minute Instagram video told viewers that people have been told for years that an independent vote is a wasted vote, and asked them to rethink that. Clinton, speaking on Kara Swisher's "High Stakes" podcast on Thursday, called the video "really unfortunate."

Read More

Clinton said the timing was all wrong. With the midterms just two weeks away on November 3, she argued voters should be backing Democrats so Republicans can be defeated, not splitting their support. The podcast clip moved fast online and became one of the most talked-about political moments of the week.

McConaughey posted his video on October 18. Its message was simple: voters should stop limiting themselves to the two big parties and give independent candidates a real look. Clinton's criticism turned that message into a debate about whether celebrity voices help campaigns or pull them off track in the final days of a race.

Fox News reported that it asked a representative for McConaughey for comment and received no response. The back and forth added to a string of public clashes this election season between Democratic figures and well-known names outside politics.

The midterms are set for November 3, and the final two weeks are likely to bring more of these clashes as both sides make their closing pitches to voters. Whether the extra attention turns into real support for independent candidates is the question now sitting underneath the noise.
