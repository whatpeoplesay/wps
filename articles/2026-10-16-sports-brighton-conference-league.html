Brighton beat Kauno Zalgiris 4-0 to start Conference League campaign

Georginio Rutter scored twice as Brighton opened their UEFA Conference League campaign with a 4-0 home win over Kauno Zalgiris.

16 October 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: Malay Mail / AFP, TNT Sports

Brighton beat Kauno Zalgiris 4-0 at home on Thursday to start their UEFA Conference League campaign with a win. The Lithuanian side were Brighton's first opponents in the league phase.

Read More

Georginio Rutter put Brighton ahead late in the first half. He finished a cutback from Ibrahim Osman, and the goal stood after a VAR review showed Osman had kept the ball in play.

Rutter then set up Jack Hinshelwood for Brighton's second goal just before half-time. Rutter scored his second goal of the night in the 53rd minute to make it 3-0.

Matt O'Riley headed in the fourth goal. It was his first goal in more than a year. The win extended Brighton's winning run in all competitions to five matches.

Brighton reached the league phase after beating Norwegian side Tromso 4-0 in the play-off round in August. Their only previous European campaign ended in the last 16 of the 2023-24 Europa League. Brighton are seen as favourites in the Conference League and hope to become the third English winner in as many years, after Chelsea and Crystal Palace. West Ham also won the trophy in 2023.
