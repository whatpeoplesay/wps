White House press secretary Katie Zacharia says she did not pass the bar exam

Katie Zacharia said on Thursday she did not pass the bar exam and never took it a second time. The answer came after days of scrutiny of her legal background.

16 October 2026 | Politics | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, USA TODAY

New White House press secretary Katie Zacharia said on Thursday she did not pass the bar exam, after facing scrutiny about her legal career before becoming the top spokeswoman for United States President Donald Trump. Zacharia joined the White House on Monday. She had previously said she practiced law before entering politics, and Trump had described her as an attorney.

Read More

In an interview on Fox News' Hannity show late on Thursday, she was asked: You did not pass the bar exam, correct? She answered: Correct. When the host, Sean Hannity, asked if she ever took the bar exam a second time, she said: I did not.

Zacharia earned her law degree from Pepperdine University in 2011, according to the university. Nearly all licensed United States lawyers first obtain a law degree, but law school graduates cannot legally practice law in the United States without first being admitted to a state bar.

On Tuesday, during her first briefing, she took questions from just three reporters before ending the event in under 15 minutes. Her performance was criticized by some media commentators and political analysts. Trump told reporters: I thought she was very good.

When Hannity asked about her performance, she said she felt she had really stepped up to hold the press conference with very little preparation time. The interview gave her a chance to answer the bar exam question directly, after days of scrutiny of her legal background.
