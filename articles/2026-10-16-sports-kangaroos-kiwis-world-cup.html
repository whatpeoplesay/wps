Bostock hat-trick on debut lifts Kangaroos past Kiwis 24-22 in World Cup opener

Jack Bostock scored three tries on his Australia debut, including the winner with four minutes left, as the Kangaroos edged New Zealand in Sydney.

16 October 2026 | Sports | By WPS Staff

Sources: NRL.com, Sky Sports

Australia beat New Zealand 24-22 in the opening match of the Rugby League World Cup at Allianz Stadium in Sydney. Jack Bostock scored a hat-trick on his debut to lead the Kangaroos to the win, in front of a crowd of 41,255.

New Zealand struck first in the fourth minute when Casey McLean scored in the corner, and Jamayne Isaako converted for a 6-0 lead. Bostock answered in the 20th minute when he intercepted a Jahrome Hughes pass and ran 88 metres to score. Tom Trbojevic then intercepted a pass and ran 85 metres to put Australia ahead.

The teams went to half-time level at 10-10. Kotoni Staggs was sent to the sin bin just before the break, and Isaako kicked the penalty goal that tied the score. Australia lost halfback Mitchell Moses to a calf injury early in the second half.

Kalyn Ponga scored twice for New Zealand in the second half. His second try, in the 60th minute, put the Kiwis ahead. But he left the field 10 minutes later with a suspected broken arm. Bostock scored his second try from a Cameron Munster kick, and Reece Walsh scored from 80 metres out.

With New Zealand leading 22-20, Bostock out-jumped Isaako to complete his hat-trick with four minutes left. Bostock ran for 231 metres and was named man of the match. He is the first Kangaroo to score three tries on debut since Campbell Graham against Scotland at the 2022 World Cup. The win was Australia's 19th in a row at World Cups.
