Netflix names Peter Rice as chief content officer

Netflix appointed former Disney executive Peter Rice as chief content officer, succeeding Bela Bajaria, who is leaving after nearly a decade at the company.

16 October 2026 | Culture | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, Marketing Mind, reporting Variety

Netflix said on Thursday it appointed former Disney executive Peter Rice as chief content officer, succeeding Bela Bajaria, who is leaving after nearly a decade at the streaming platform.

Read More

Rice will oversee Netflix's content strategy from October 26, including series, films and live events across regions. Previously, he served as chairman of Walt Disney Television and chairman of general entertainment at The Walt Disney Company.

Bajaria joined Netflix in 2016 and was named chief content officer in January 2023. During her tenure, Netflix released pop-culture hits such as Bridgerton and Squid Game and expanded live programming through multi-year partnerships with WWE and the NFL.

The announcement comes a day after Netflix told staff in an internal memo that it was laying off 6 percent of its workforce, roughly 1,000 employees, marking its biggest job cuts since 2022.

The streaming giant has faced doubts over its ability to sustain growth as engagement becomes a key concern for investors, with platforms such as YouTube and TikTok attracting viewers through free, user-generated content that gives them a cost advantage over Netflix.
