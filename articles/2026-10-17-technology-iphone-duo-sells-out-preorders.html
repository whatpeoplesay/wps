Apple's first foldable iPhone Duo sells out within minutes of pre-orders opening

Pre-orders for Apple's first foldable iPhone sold out within minutes on Friday, pushing delivery estimates from 23 October to late January.

17 October 2026 | Technology | By WPS Staff

Sources: iThinkDiff, Analytics Insight

Apple says demand for its first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, has been nothing short of extraordinary after pre-orders opened on Friday, 16 October and sold out within minutes. Delivery estimates on Apple's online store in the United States slipped from launch day, 23 October, to 25 January for every configuration.

Read More

Buyers who ordered the moment the store opened at 5 a.m. Pacific still got delivery on launch day. Within minutes, estimates slipped into November, and half an hour after the store opened, every order showed 25 January. The January date applied to all colours, star white and night sky, and all storage sizes, 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB.

The phone starts at $1,999 for 256GB, rising to $2,199 for 512GB, $2,599 for 1TB and $3,199 for 2TB. Apple has not said how many units it had ready at launch or when supply will catch up. The company said it is working to get the phone to buyers as quickly as it can.

Supply was reported to be tight before pre-orders opened. One supply chain report said final assembly yield for the phone was only slightly above 60 percent in mid-September, and put Apple's 2026 stocking target at roughly six million to eight million units. Wider component shortages, especially in memory chips, have also pressed the supply chain.

Walk-in availability on launch day, 23 October, is still unconfirmed. Store pickup was briefly offered during pre-order and then switched off, and Apple's site tells buyers to check back on 24 October. Resellers moved fast: a 2TB night sky model listed at $3,199 sold on eBay for $5,000, and a confirmed pre-order for a 1TB night sky model sold for $4,400 against Apple's $2,599 price.
