OpenAI Nears $30 Billion Raise at $1.25 Trillion Valuation

OpenAI is close to raising up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation in a round led by Coatue and UAE investors, ahead of a possible stock market listing.

24 October 2026 | Technology | By WPS Staff

Sources: TechCrunch, Bloomberg (via The AI Pin)

OpenAI is close to raising up to $30 billion in a new private funding round, according to people familiar with the talks cited by CNBC. The round would value the ChatGPT maker at $1.25 trillion before the new money is counted, and it is being led by the investment firm Coatue together with investors from the United Arab Emirates.

Read More

The figure is lower than what was reported a month ago, when Bloomberg said OpenAI was seeking the same $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation. People familiar with the matter say the talks are still going on and the final terms could change. OpenAI declined to comment on the reports.

The UAE side of the deal is not only about money. CNBC reports that the funding could come with a separate arrangement giving OpenAI access to more computing capacity from the UAE. That matters because building and running AI models takes enormous computing power, and access to it shapes how fast a company can grow.

If the round closes above $1 trillion, OpenAI would become the most valuable private company in history. It would also set a very high bar for the company's planned stock market listing. OpenAI filed confidential paperwork for an IPO in June, after its March round valued the company at $852 billion post-money.

The news arrived in a week when investors were selling technology shares over worries about the cost of the AI boom. CNBC also reports that rival Anthropic is aiming for a $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion valuation in its own public debut, after raising $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation in May.
