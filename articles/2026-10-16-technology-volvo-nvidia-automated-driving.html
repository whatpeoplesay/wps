Volvo and Nvidia expand partnership for hands-free driving

Volvo Cars and Nvidia expanded their automated driving partnership. The new system will start with supervised driving features and move toward hands-free driving over time.

16 October 2026 | Technology | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, Cryptopolitan

Volvo Cars and Nvidia are expanding their partnership on automated driving. The two companies said on Thursday they will build a new generation of driver assistance and automated driving technology for future Volvo cars.

Read More

Volvo will bring its safety systems and engineering work. Nvidia will bring its AI and computing technology, including the Hyperion sensor and computer setup, the Halos safety layer and the Alpamayo driving software. The aim is a system that can handle highways, city streets and parking, from door to door.

The first rollout will focus on supervised driving features. Drivers will still be responsible for the car. Volvo's aim is to add more advanced features over time, including hands-free driving in cities and on highways.

The technology should reach Volvo cars before 2030. It will launch in several regions at the same time, but not in China. Volvo has not yet set a price model, and the features will not be limited to higher-priced cars.

Nvidia also works with other carmakers, including Mercedes, Jaguar Land Rover and Hyundai. Nvidia automotive chief Ali Kani said safety is the most important job of automated driving assistance technology, and that working with Volvo would make the system safer.
