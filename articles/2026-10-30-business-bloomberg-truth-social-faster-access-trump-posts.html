Bloomberg and trading firms pay for faster access to Trump posts

Bloomberg and two Wall Street trading firms have signed up for faster access to Donald Trump's posts on Truth Social, paying up to $100,000 a month for a head start that everyday investors do not get.

30 October 2026 | Business | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, BeInCrypto

Truth Social has signed up news and data provider Bloomberg and two Wall Street firms for faster access to President Donald Trump's posts, according to people with direct knowledge of the matter. The service sells for up to $100,000 a month. The firms are Susquehanna International Group and Jump Trading, both high-frequency trading firms that use automated strategies.

The service, called Truth API, gives subscribers a timing advantage on Trump's posts, which can move financial markets. Trump Media and Technology Group, which owns Truth Social, announced its fastest-access product in July and pitched it to trading firms and other financial institutions. The timing advantage, described as 50 milliseconds by Trump Media officials, can matter because a president's posts can create swings in the value of assets.

The deals are likely the first time a business tied to a sitting president has sold access to that president's statements, and some critics have called the arrangement unethical. Democratic Senators Elizabeth Warren and Adam Schiff said in July that they had asked the US Securities and Exchange Commission to investigate whether the service violated securities laws, describing it as a shocking abuse of office. The SEC declined to comment.

The paid-access plan is already facing a legal challenge. In August, Intercept Media and the Freedom of the Press Foundation sued Trump, seeking to block the White House from posting official government announcements only on Truth Social while the paid feed exists. The suit says putting a financial barrier in front of government information is potentially unconstitutional and favours wealthy Wall Street firms.

Trump Media said Truth Social offers an industry-standard API product for which there is significant demand across industries, and said the reporting was based on misleading information from anonymous sources. The White House said neither Trump nor his family is directly involved in portfolio investment decisions. Bloomberg, Jump Trading and Susquehanna declined to comment, while a family trust owns 41 percent of Trump Media, with Trump as its sole beneficiary.
