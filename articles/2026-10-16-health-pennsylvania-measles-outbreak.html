Pennsylvania measles outbreak reaches 1,222 cases, state says

Pennsylvania reported 1,222 measles cases across 42 counties, with 225 hospitalizations and five confirmed measles-associated deaths, in its latest statewide update.

16 October 2026 | Health | By WPS Staff

Sources: Pennsylvania Department of Health, Reuters

The Pennsylvania Department of Health reported 1,222 measles cases in the state, up 86 cases from a week earlier. The cases span 42 counties, and the state has recorded 225 hospitalizations and five confirmed measles-associated deaths this year.

Read More

The state said fewer than 1 percent of the cases, four in total, were in vaccinated people. The department is also investigating a recently reported death in Franklin County. To count as measles-associated, the state requires symptoms plus a positive lab test, and a death within 30 days of symptom onset that was not due to an unrelated cause.

Nationally, the CDC counted 4,080 confirmed measles cases as of October 8, reported by 47 jurisdictions and including 18 cases among international visitors. About 96 percent were linked to outbreaks. The CDC has confirmed two measles-associated deaths nationwide in 2026, without identifying the states involved, a lower count than the state's figure because state and federal definitions differ.

The CDC said it would deploy additional support to Pennsylvania to help investigate the outbreak, after the state formally requested federal help through the agency's Epi-Aid emergency response program. The agency said it would work with local health officials to investigate the outbreak, strengthen response efforts, and prevent spread.

The state has added an epidemic curve to its measles dashboard, showing cases over time by week so residents can see whether transmission is rising, steady, or falling. Health officials say the MMR vaccine remains the main protection, with one dose giving about 93 percent protection against measles and two doses about 97 percent, and they advise anyone with possible symptoms to call a doctor's office before going in.
