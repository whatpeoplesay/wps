Nasdaq plunges 5.52% as AI stocks crash

The Nasdaq fell 5.52% on Thursday in its worst day in 19 months, wiping about $2.5 trillion from the index as AI stocks led a broad sell-off.

6 November 2026 | Technology | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters via Times of India, Firstpost

The Nasdaq fell 5.52% on Thursday, its worst day in 19 months and its biggest one-day drop since the tariff sell-off in April 2025. The Nasdaq 100 lost about $2.5 trillion in market value in a single day, and the index closed in correction territory, more than 10% below the record high it reached on 6 October.

Read More

The wider market fell too. The S&P 500 dropped 4.36% and the Dow fell 3.44%, with about $3 trillion wiped off the value of S&P 500 companies. Chip and AI stocks led the losses: Nvidia fell 9% and AMD fell 17%, while the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 12% to its lowest level in more than six months.

CoreWeave fell 10% and Nebius fell 15%, while Applied Digital, Cipher and IREN each dropped 14% or more. GE Vernova fell 7.4%, Microsoft fell 3.6%, and Amazon and Alphabet each fell 2.2%.

The sell-off followed the Democrats' wins in the midterm elections, which raised fears of closer scrutiny and opposition that could slow the building of data centres. UBS estimated that about 30% of the data-centre capacity additions expected in the United States in 2028 could be at risk. Voters in at least 38 jurisdictions approved bans or restrictions on data centres, and Gina Hinojosa, the governor-elect of Texas, pledged to halt approvals for new data-centre projects.

Ted Parkhill, the founder and chief executive of Incline Investment Management, said: "The market's moving from the promise of AI to the price of AI." Even after Thursday's fall, the Nasdaq is still up 4.6% in 2026.
