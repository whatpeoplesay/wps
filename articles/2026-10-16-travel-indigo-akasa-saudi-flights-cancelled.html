IndiGo and Akasa Air extend Saudi Arabia flight cancellations

Indian airlines IndiGo and Akasa Air have extended flight suspensions to Saudi Arabia amid escalating regional tensions and disruptions to air travel. IndiGo cancellations now run until October 24.

16 October 2026 | Travel | By WPS Staff

Sources: The Economic Times, The New Indian Express

Indian airlines IndiGo and Akasa Air have extended flight suspensions to Saudi Arabia amid escalating regional tensions and disruptions to air travel. Air India had earlier cancelled flights to and from three Saudi cities until October 17, 2026.

Read More

IndiGo has cancelled all flights to and from Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Madinah until October 24, 2026, inclusive, citing the evolving situation in Saudi Arabia. The airline said it was sending timely notifications to affected customers using the contact details provided at the time of booking.

Akasa Air has extended the suspension of all flights to and from Riyadh until October 24 and Jeddah until October 21, 2026. In its October 15 travel update, the airline cited the prevailing security situation in the Middle East and said it was closely monitoring developments and assessing the situation in real time.

Passengers affected by the cancellations can opt for a full refund, which will be credited to the original source of payment within seven days, or reschedule their travel at no additional cost. Those who booked through travel partners have been asked to contact them directly for assistance.

Air India had earlier announced on October 13 that all its flights to and from Riyadh, Dammam and Jeddah would remain cancelled until October 17, 2026. The airline said it was closely monitoring and assessing developments in the region to resume safe operations, and it is offering affected passengers a full refund on cancellation.
