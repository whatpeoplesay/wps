TSMC profit jumps 69% to a record as AI chip demand stays strong

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company reported a record third-quarter profit on October 15. Revenue also hit a record, beating the top of the range the company had set for itself in July.

16 October 2026 | Business | By WPS Staff

Sources: Global Brands Magazine, Reuters

TSMC reported third-quarter revenue of NT$1,494.24 billion, or US$46.72 billion, on October 15, 2026. Revenue was up 50.9 percent year on year in Taiwan dollars and 41.2 percent in US dollars. It beat the top of the company's own US$44.6 billion to US$45.8 billion guidance. Net income rose 68.7 percent to NT$763.06 billion, a record for the sixth quarter in a row.

Profitability stayed wide. Gross margin came in at 66.4 percent, down 1.3 percentage points from the second quarter but above the 65 percent to 67 percent range the company had guided to. Operating margin was 58.7 percent, also above the 56 percent to 58 percent guidance. Diluted earnings per share were NT$29.43, or about US$4.60 per American depositary receipt.

High-performance computing, the category that holds AI accelerators, was 68 percent of revenue. Smartphones, once TSMC's largest market, were 21 percent. The 3-nanometre node was 33 percent of wafer revenue, and the new 2-nanometre node, in volume production for only a few months, was already 8 percent. Five-nanometre production was 28 percent and 7-nanometre was 10 percent, so advanced nodes of 7-nanometre and below were roughly 79 percent of wafer revenue in total.

Chief executive C.C. Wei told analysts the gap between demand and supply is still quite big and that the company did not see any sign of slowdown. Chief financial officer Wendell Huang told the call that capital spending in the 2026 to 2028 period would be even more significantly higher than the past three years. Capital spending for 2026 was held at US$60 billion to US$64 billion.

Guidance for the fourth quarter is US$52.3 billion to US$54.3 billion in revenue, about 14 percent higher again at the midpoint, with a gross margin of 65 percent to 67 percent and an operating margin of 56.5 percent to 58.5 percent. For the full year the company expects revenue growth of about 44 percent in US dollar terms.
