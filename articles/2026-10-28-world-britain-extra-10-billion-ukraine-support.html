Britain gives extra £10 billion support to Ukraine

Britain announced £10 billion in extra support for Ukraine on 28 October, including £500 million in urgent military funding for this winter.

28 October 2026 | World | By WPS Staff

Sources: Reuters, Kyiv Independent

Britain announced £10 billion in extra support for Ukraine on Wednesday, 28 October. Finance minister John Healey told lawmakers about the package as he presented the budget. The support is meant to help Ukraine in its war with Russia.

Read More

Healey said the money would be based on loans, export finance guarantees, and £500 million in urgent military funding for this winter. Downing Street said £7 billion would be economic support over three years, and £3 billion would be export finance capacity for Ukraine.

A government release also listed £5.5 billion in World Bank guarantees and £500 million for the World Bank crisis facility. The £3 billion in export finance would prioritise military support for Ukraine. The £500 million in winter military support is aimed at plugging urgent gaps in Ukraine air defences.

The announcement came as Ukraine partners face pressure to step up their support for 2027. Ukraine faces a 70 billion euro shortfall for 2027, even after 45 billion euros of planned support from the European Union. Norway had already pledged $9 billion for 2027, and the European Union is still considering its own options for next year.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham called it the biggest financial package Britain has ever committed to Ukraine. President Volodymyr Zelensky called it a truly strong and very timely step, and said he was grateful to Britain. Healey said he and Burnham had spoken to Zelensky before the budget, and Zelensky told them, "you've made my day".
