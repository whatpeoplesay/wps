Obama hits back at Trump over Cheesecake Factory row at Georgia rally

Former President Barack Obama told a Georgia rally on 28 October that the Trump administration answered his Cheesecake Factory joke with a full government response, instead of answering his criticism on prices and living costs.

29 October 2026 | US | By WPS Staff

Sources: Washington Examiner, TheWrap

Former President Barack Obama spoke at a campaign rally in Georgia on Wednesday, 28 October, alongside Senator Jon Ossoff and gubernatorial candidate Keisha Lance Bottoms. He said President Donald Trump and his administration had reacted more strongly to a joke about the White House than to his criticism of rising prices and living costs.

Read More

The joke came from an earlier campaign stop, where Obama said the White House now looked as if Saddam Hussein had opened a Cheesecake Factory, pointing to the gold decoration and a new ballroom. Trump answered by calling Obama stupid and defending the renovation work, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent later appeared outside a Cheesecake Factory restaurant after the remark drew attention.

At the Georgia rally, Obama said the administration had not disputed his claim that it was directly responsible for rising prices, had not argued against his point that tariffs were adding to costs for families, and had not denied his charge of corruption. He said the Saddam Hussein comparison had not bothered Trump, but the Cheesecake Factory comment had upset him greatly.

Obama said the response showed the administration was not serious, had no answers to real problems, and preferred talk about the restaurant to talk about higher bills. He said the point was that prices had risen so much that many people could barely afford to eat out, while officials spent their time on the joke instead.

The rally was held in the Atlanta area, in Morrow and at the Arena at Southlake, in the final week before next week's election, with thousands of people in the crowd. Obama told voters they could vote for change in Georgia, and the event was held in support of Ossoff and Bottoms.
